2. THE SHORT VERSION
================================================================================

- We collect what you give us (your account, profile, messages and posts), what
  your device tells us (for example your location, if you allow it) and what
  happens when you use GreenGo (for example purchases and reports).
- Other users see your public profile: your photos, your name or nickname, your
  age and an APPROXIMATE location that is accurate only to about 1.5 km. If
  you choose "Exact" in Globe Discoverability, they see a point within about
  100 m of your position instead. They never see your exact coordinates, date
  of birth, phone number or email address.
- Since 16 October 2026, to create an account you must verify a mobile phone
  number by SMS (older accounts do not have to add one). Your number is
  private and never shown to other users. If we ban an account, we keep
  only a keyed hash of its number, to stop it from coming back.
- We do NOT sell your personal data. We do NOT use it for third-party
  advertising. There are no ads in GreenGo.
- Your messages are encrypted in transit and at rest, but they are NOT
  end-to-end encrypted.
  - Our systems can process message content, for example to translate it when
    you ask, or to check a message someone has reported.
- Verifying with an identity document is optional, except where the law
  requires stronger age checks (section 8.1). If you do it, you type in the
  details from the document, a reviewer checks them against the image, and
  we then delete the image. We keep the reviewed details encrypted in a
  restricted store, only to identify you when the law requires it, until
  6 months after your account is closed.
- AI features (translation, read-aloud and transliteration of messages
  written in non-Latin scripts) use Google services, and our support
  assistant uses Anthropic's Claude. They run through our servers, except
  that the app may ask Google's translation service directly to translate
  public content. We ask for your consent before they first process your
  private content, and the support assistant is clearly labelled as AI.
- You can delete your account at any time:
  - in the app;
  - on our website, at https://greengochat.com/delete-account, by confirming
    a link we email to you.
- You have rights over your data: access, correction, deletion, portability,
  objection and more. See section 16.

3. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT
================================================================================

3.1 Data you give us

a) Account data
   Email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our
   authentication provider), mobile phone number (verified with a code sent
   by SMS, see section 6.5), nickname and display name, date of birth, and
   the language you use.

b) Profile data
   - Photos, an optional video profile and voice introduction.
   - About you: bio, interests, the languages you speak and are learning, and
     gender.
   - Optional details you choose to add: height, education, occupation,
     personality traits, country or culture of origin, and links to your
     social media accounts.
   - We do not ask for your sexual orientation (see 3.4).
   - Your privacy choices, for example incognito mode, ghost mode, whether
     you appear on the map and whether you allow promotional use of your
     content (section 6.4).

c) Content and communications
   - Messages in one-to-one chats, groups, communities and event chats: text,
     photos, videos, voice messages and reactions.
   - Posts, tips, announcements, reviews and ratings.
   - Your event RSVPs.
   - Experiences you host or book.
   - Messages you send to our support team.

d) Business account data
   Business name and legal name, category, address shown on your page, opening
   hours, contact channels, an optional business contact number (not
   verified, it can be a landline; private unless you switch on showing it
   on your business page) and, where you ask to be verified as a business, the
   documents needed for verification (see section 8.4). If you sell tickets or
   experiences, also your billing data (see h below) and, if you act as a
   trader, your trader details (see j below).

e) Verification data
   An identity document and/or a selfie, only if you choose to verify. If
   you verify with an identity document, also the details you type in from
   it: first name, surname, date of birth, document type, issuing country
   and document number. See section 8.

f) Purchase data
   - What you bought, when, the price and currency.
   - The store or payment provider used, and the order or transaction number.
   - On our website, your payment is entered on Stripe's own page. We do not
     receive your full card number.

g) Reports and appeals
   What you tell us when you report something or someone, and when you appeal
   one of our decisions.

h) Billing data of business accounts
   If you sell tickets or experiences: legal name, tax identification number,
   billing address, billing e-mail and, optionally, phone and contact person.
   See section 11.6.

i) Guest booking data
   If you book without an account (section 11.7): your first name, surname,
   email address and your booking or ticket order.

j) Trader details of sellers
   If you sell tickets or experiences and declare that you act as a trader:
   your name, address, phone number, email, identity document or
   registration number and, if you are registered, your trade register and
   number. See section 11.8.

3.2 Data created when you use GreenGo

a) Location
   If you allow it, your device's location. See section 7 for exactly what is
   stored and what others see.

b) Device and technical data
   - Device model, operating system and app version, language and time zone.
   - IP address.

c) Usage data
   - The features you use.

   - Your coin and membership history.
   - Your achievements and streaks.
   - Whether you are online and when you were last seen.

d) Analytics data
   - In-app events and performance measurements, linked to a pseudonymous

3.3 Data we receive from others

a) Other users: reports about you, ratings and reviews of you as a host or

b) Apple and Google: confirmation of the purchases you make through their

c) Stripe: confirmation of web payments, refunds and payment disputes.

d) Sign-in providers: if we offer sign-in with Google or Apple and you use

3.4 Sensitive data

Some data is "sensitive" (special category) under the law. We collect it only
in these cases:

- Sexual orientation: we do NOT collect it. Earlier versions of the app had
  an optional, private field for it. That field has been removed; any value
  saved earlier was never shown to other users and is deleted.

- Identity document and selfie: only if you choose to verify, or need to in
  the cases described in section 8. We ask for your explicit consent at the
  moment of upload.

- Content you share: messages or posts may reveal sensitive information, for
  example your religion or health, if you choose to write about it. We do not
  analyse your content to infer sensitive traits.

We do not ask for your racial or ethnic origin. The optional "country or
culture of origin" field describes nationality or cultural background. You
decide whether to fill it in.

Your exact coordinates, date of birth, email address, phone number, identity
document and the identity details you gave from it, verification selfie and
private-album photos (unless you grant someone access to your private album).

Who follows you, whom you follow, and your following and store counts. This
applies to personal and business accounts. Other users can follow you and see
your number of followers (4.1), but only you see who they are.

4.3 Content you share

- Messages are visible to the people in that conversation, group, community or
  event chat.
- Posts in a public community are visible to everyone who can open it.
- Your business page and the experiences you publish are public. Your
  business contact number is shown on your business page only if you switch
  this on. The verified phone number of your account is never shown.
- If you sell as a trader, your trader details are shown on your listings
  (section 11.8).

4.4 Our staff

A small number of authorised staff can access account data when it is needed,
for example to:
- handle a report or an appeal;
- answer a support request;
- review a verification;
- meet a legal obligation.

Access to identity documents, and to the encrypted identity details we keep
after the review (section 8.2), is limited to a small number of authorised
senior administrators. Every access is logged.

7. LOCATION
================================================================================

7.1 What we collect

If you allow location access, the app reads your device's location while you
are using it. We never collect location in the background. If you are a
traveller, you can also set the place you are travelling to. On the web, if
your browser gives no location, the app may estimate your city from your IP
address. You can always correct it.

7.2 What we store

We store your exact location in a private part of your account that only you
and our systems can access. We use it to calculate distances and to find
people, events and experiences near you.

7.3 What others see

By default, other users see only your city and country, and an approximate
point that is within about 1.5 km of your real position. Distances shown to
others are based on approximate data.

If you set Globe Discoverability to "Exact", other users see a point within
about 100 m of your position, and distances to you rounded to the nearest
100 m. The point is your last saved location and is updated when you move to
a different area (more than about 1 km). You can switch this off at any time
in Settings by choosing another option; the point is then removed at once.

Your exact coordinates are never shown to other users.

7.4 Who else receives it

To turn coordinates into a city name, we use Google's geocoding service. Maps
inside the app are loaded from map providers (OpenStreetMap, CARTO, Google
Maps), which receive your device's IP address and the map area you view, not
your account data.

7.5 Your choices

- You can turn location access off at any time in your device settings, or
  set your city manually.
- Some features, such as "near me", will then be less accurate.

8. AGE VERIFICATION, IDENTITY DOCUMENTS AND SELFIES
================================================================================

8.1 When we ask

GreenGo is for adults (18+). Everyone states their date of birth when they sign
up. Verifying with an identity document is optional, also for publishing in
a Community and for publishing or booking experiences. We may ask for an
identity document only:
- where the law requires stronger age checks where you live;
- when we have a specific reason to believe an account belongs to someone
  under 18.

8.2 What happens to your document

a) Before you upload, we explain what will happen and ask for your explicit
   consent.

b) After uploading the image, you type in your first name, surname, date of
   birth, document type, issuing country and document number.

c) For age verification, the image is sent to Google Cloud Vision, which
   reads the text on the document so that we can find the date of birth.
   Google acts on our behalf and keeps nothing afterwards.

d) A trained member of our staff checks the details you typed in against
   the image of the document, and confirms or corrects them. Details that
   are not reviewed within 30 days are DELETED.

e) The image is DELETED as soon as the review is finished. If it is still
   waiting for review after 7 days, it is DELETED whatever the outcome, and
   you can upload it again. This applies to every identity-document image,
   including the photo of you holding your identity document that you can
   add when you sign up (8.3) and the identity document of a business owner
   (8.4).

f) After the review we keep:
   - whether your age was verified (yes or no), the method, the date of the
     check and your year of birth;
   - the reviewed details (first name, surname, date of birth, document
     type, issuing country and document number), ENCRYPTED and kept apart
     from your profile, in a restricted store.

   We do not keep the image, your nationality or the document's expiry date.

g) We use the encrypted details only to identify you when the law requires
   it: to answer a valid order from a court, or a request from the police or
   another competent authority; to protect someone's life or safety in a
   serious case; or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims (section
   12.5). Only a small number of authorised staff can access them, and every
   access is logged. We never show them to other users, and we never use them
   for anything else, for example marketing or profiling.

h) We keep the encrypted details while your account exists and for 6 months
   after it is closed (the period for which the Brazilian Marco Civil da
   Internet requires access logs to be kept, art. 15). We keep them longer
   only under a legal hold (section 14).

8.3 Selfie verification

You can verify your profile by taking a selfie. A member of our staff compares
it with your profile photos. We delete the selfie after the review and keep only
the result (verified or not). We do not create a face template or any other
biometric identifier from your selfie, and we do not use facial recognition to
identify you. A photo of you holding your identity document is an
identity-document image: the rules in 8.2, including deletion after 7 days at
the latest, apply to it.

On your device, before you upload a photo, the app checks that a face is
visible and screens it for nudity. This check runs entirely on your phone,
using Google ML Kit. Nothing is sent to us or to Google for it, and no face
data is stored.

8.4 Business verification

A business account does not need an identity document. To verify a
business account we may ask for the business's registration
details. An identity document of the person who runs it is optional. If the
owner provides one, it follows the same rules as in 8.2: the image is deleted
after the review (at the latest after 7 days), and the confirmed details are
kept encrypted only for legal requests. We keep the business
registration details for as long as the business page exists and for 6 months
afterwards, because the EU Digital Services Act requires us to be able to
identify traders who offer services to consumers.

8.5 If you say no

You can refuse or withdraw your consent to verification. You then cannot use
the features listed in 8.1 that need it. Your account is not deleted. The
identity details you already gave are kept as described in 8.2 g and h.

8.6 Notice for residents of Illinois, Texas and other US states with biometric
laws

We do not collect "biometric identifiers" or "biometric information", that is
scans of face geometry, from your identity document or your selfie:
- the document is only read for its text;
- the selfie is compared visually by a person.

If we ever introduce automated facial comparison, we will first:
- tell you in writing what we collect and why, and how long we keep it;
- obtain your written release (consent);
- publish a retention schedule. Under that schedule, biometric data is
  destroyed when the purpose is met, or within 3 years of your last
  interaction with us, whichever comes first.

We will never sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from biometric data.

- AI requests go from our servers to the provider. The only exception is
  the translation of public content: if it is not in our shared store, the
  app may request it from Google's translation service directly. Your
  private content is never sent from your device to an AI provider.
- For your private content, the providers act as our processors. We use them
  under their paid business terms, under which they do not use your data to
  train their models.
- Before AI features process your private content for the first time (for
  example translation of chat messages, read-aloud and the AI assistant), we
  show you which provider is used and what is sent, and we ask for your
  consent. You can change this in Settings.
- Public content, such as events, communities and listings, is translated
  with Google's translation service through a shared store on our servers.
  Only the text is sent, without any personal data, and the translation is
  stored for everyone who views that content. This does not need your
  consent.
- Translations of chat messages are stored with the message, so that they do
  not have to be requested again. Translations of short phrases with no
  personal details (no numbers, links, email addresses or @names) are also
  kept in an anonymous shared cache for up to 180 days, without the original
  message or any link to you.
- Content created or changed by AI is labelled, for example "Translated".
  AI output can contain mistakes. Do not rely on it for important decisions.
- Our support chat first answers with an AI assistant, which is clearly
  labelled as AI. You can tap "Talk to a person" at any time.
- When you translate a message from someone else, their text is sent to the
  translation provider. They agreed to this possibility when they accepted
  this policy.
- Read-aloud: short audio created from a text may be kept in an anonymous
  shared cache, so that the same text does not have to be converted again.
  The cache stores only the text and its audio, without your account id or
  any other link to you.

9.3 Automated decisions

- detecting nudity or violence in images;
- spotting spam and scams;
- checking that the date of birth on a document agrees with the one you gave;

- Most automated results are reviewed by a person before any serious action is
  taken against your account.
- Where an automated decision does affect you, we tell you, give you the
  reasons and let you appeal to a human (section 10.4).
- You can always ask for a human review of a decision made solely by automated
  means (GDPR art. 22; LGPD art. 20).

10. MESSAGES, SAFETY AND MODERATION
================================================================================

10.1 Security of messages

- Messages are encrypted while they travel between your device and our
  servers, and while they are stored.
- They are NOT end-to-end encrypted. This means our systems can technically
  access message content. We use this access only to:
  - deliver and store your messages;
  - translate them or read them aloud when you or the other person ask;
  - back them up when you ask;
  - check content that someone has reported, or that our automated safety
    tools flag;
  - meet a legal obligation.

10.2 Reports

- When you report a person or content, we create a case in our moderation
  queue. The case includes your report and a copy of the reported content.
- Reports about child safety are handled first.
- We tell you when we have reviewed your report.
- If someone often sends clearly unfounded reports, we keep a count of their
  unfounded reports for 12 months. We warn them and, if they continue, a
  moderator reviews the case and may stop processing their reports for 30
  days (DSA art. 23; Terms and Conditions, section 18.6). Reports about child
  safety are always processed.

10.3 Child sexual exploitation

- We have zero tolerance for child sexual exploitation and abuse material
  (CSAM).
- We use tools to detect known CSAM.
- We report apparent CSAM to the US National Center for Missing & Exploited
  Children (NCMEC) and to the competent authorities, as the law requires. We
  keep the related data for the period the law requires for the
  investigation (at least one year under US law, 18 U.S.C. 2258A).

10.4 Statement of reasons and appeals

- If we remove your content or restrict your account, we send you a notice in
  the app and by email. It says what we did, which content it concerns, why,
  which rule it broke and whether automated means were used.
- We send these notices, and the emails about your appeal, to your account
  email address even if your account is suspended.
- You can appeal within 6 months, in the app or through the link in the
  notice email. A person who was not involved in the original decision
  reviews the appeal, and we send you the outcome and the reasons.
- If you reported something and we decided not to act, you can appeal that
  decision in the same way.
- More details are in our Terms and Conditions.

10.5 Private photos and chat media: warning and record

- Copying or sharing other users' private photos, private-album content, chat
  media or personal data without their consent is forbidden (Terms and
  Conditions, section 16.9).
- The first time you open such content, the app shows a warning that you
  must confirm ("I understand"). We record your account id, the date and
  time, and the version of the Terms you confirmed. We use this record only
  to prove that you were informed, for example in a moderation case, an
  appeal, or a request from the police or a court.
- The legal basis is our legitimate interest, and that of the people whose
  content you see, in preventing and proving misuse (GDPR 6(1)(f); LGPD 7
  IX). We keep the record as described in section 14.

15. DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT
================================================================================

15.1 How to delete

- In the app: Profile > Delete Account. You will be asked to sign in again to
  confirm.
- On the web: https://greengochat.com/delete-account. Enter your email address,
  and we will send you a confirmation link that is valid for 24 hours. Your
  account is deleted when you confirm. For your security, the page tells
  nobody whether an account exists for that email.

15.2 What happens

a) Your profile disappears from GreenGo immediately.

b) We delete your account, your profile, your photos, videos and voice
   recordings, your settings, your memberships, your community, group and
   event memberships, your verification results, your private location data,
   your saved items and your other personal records.

c) Messages you sent are removed for everyone. Other people in the
   conversation see "Message deleted" in their place. Media you sent is
   deleted.

d) Content that belongs to a shared space stays for its members, but without
   your identity. For example, a community you created stays for its members.

e) Purchase and payment records are kept for the period the tax and accounting
   laws require (see section 14). We first remove your name, email and account
   id from them and replace the id with a code that cannot identify you
   without additional information.
   Fee invoices issued to a business account, and the billing data on them,
   are kept unchanged for the period tax law requires, because an invoice
   must show the seller's legal details.

f) We also ask our providers to delete your data:
   - Stripe deletes your customer record;
   - your analytics and crash data are deleted;
   - your email contact record is deleted.

g) Backups are overwritten within 90 days.

h) We keep a short deletion receipt (the date and the number of items removed)
   so that we can prove the deletion took place.

i) Your phone number is deleted with your account. The encrypted identity
   details from an identity-document verification are kept for 6 months after
   the account is closed, and then deleted (section 8.2 h). If the account
   was banned, we keep the keyed hashes of its email address and phone number
   (section 14).

15.3 Any coins left in your account and any remaining membership time are lost
when you delete your account. Deleting the app does not delete your account.
Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Apple or
Google: cancel it in your store account first.

20. SECURITY AND DATA BREACHES
================================================================================

20.1 Our security measures

- Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.
- Access rules that limit each record to the people who need it.
- Server-side checks for purchases and coins.
- App Check against fake clients.
- Restricted, logged administrator access.
- Identity details from document verification kept encrypted, in a separate

- Keys and secrets kept in a secrets manager.
- Regular security reviews.

No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong, unique password and keep
your device up to date.

20.2 If a breach happens

If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will:
a) notify the competent data protection authority:
