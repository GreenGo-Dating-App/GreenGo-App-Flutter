- cancelling it in good time if it cannot go ahead.

11.2 Free and paid Events
a) Joining a free Event is free. The organiser may let you bring plus-ones
   (guests). Each plus-one takes one place: the capacity counts you and your
   guests.
b) Only active Business accounts (section 13) can sell tickets for their
   Events. The ticket contract is between you and the organiser: the
   organiser is the seller, sets the price and the cancellation and refund
   policy, and is responsible for the Event and for issuing any invoice or
   receipt and paying any taxes on the ticket. You can buy up to 4 tickets
   per Event in total. Paid Events have no plus-ones: every person needs a
   ticket.
c) Paid tickets are paid only through the in-app checkout, to the
   organiser's own Stripe or Mercado Pago account connected to GreenGo. There
   are no payment links and no other payment methods for new orders: only
   ticket orders opened before 14 October 2026 with the organiser's own
   payment method can still be completed that way. You pay the ticket price
   shown, and nothing more. The money goes to the organiser's account: GreenGo
   never holds ticket money. GreenGo receives only its service fee, which the
   organiser pays (section 11.6).
d) Buyer/guest name and email are shared with the event organizer for entry
   (see the Privacy Policy, section 11.4). If the organiser asks for attendee
   names, you must give the first name and surname of each ticket holder.
   These names are shared only with the organiser and shown on the tickets.
e) Your consumer rights against the organiser, including any right of
   withdrawal, are those of the law that applies to the organiser's sale.
   Contact the organiser first; if they do not answer, report the Event in the
   app and we will act as described in section 18.
f) Price phases: the organiser of a paid Event can sell its tickets in price
   phases (for example "Early bird", "Regular" or "At the door"). Each phase
   has its own price and number of tickets, and each phase costs more than
   the one before. When a phase sells out, the next one starts. Our server
   sets the price of each ticket at checkout, from the phase that is current
   at that moment; an order for several tickets can include tickets from
   more than one phase. The checkout shows the price before you pay.
   If the phase changes while you are paying, we show you the new price
   before you confirm. Together, the price phases cannot hold more tickets
   than the Event's capacity.

11.3 Event check-in uses a QR code in the app. For paid Events the QR code is

11.6 GreenGo service fee (paid by organisers and Hosts)
a) GreenGo charges a service fee on each paid ticket for an Event and each
   paid booking of an Experience. The organiser or Host (the "seller") pays
   it, not the buyer. Stripe (as an application fee) or Mercado Pago (as a
   marketplace fee) deducts it from the ticket price when the buyer pays, so
   the seller receives the ticket price minus the fee.
b) The fee per ticket depends on the seller's Membership at the time of the
   sale:
   - Silver: US$3 + 10% of the ticket price, at most US$30;
   - Gold: US$2 + 10% of the ticket price, at most US$28;
   - Platinum: US$1 + 10% of the ticket price, at most US$25.
   With price phases (section 11.2 f), the fee is calculated on the price of
   each ticket.
c) First paid listing: if you have not sold paid tickets on GreenGo before,
   the fee is a flat US$1 per ticket for the first listing (Event or
   Experience) on which a buyer opens a paid checkout, for that listing.
d) Free tickets have no fee. The fee is never higher than the ticket price.
e) For tickets priced in another currency, the fixed part and the maximum are
   converted from US dollars with an exchange table that GreenGo maintains.
   The 10% is calculated on the ticket price in its own currency. The app
   shows the seller an estimate of the payout before publishing.
f) Refunds: if a ticket or booking is refunded in full, the fee is returned
   to the seller. If it is refunded only in part, the buyer gets back the
   refunded amount and GreenGo keeps its fee.
g) Stripe and Mercado Pago charge the seller their own processing fees under
   their own terms. These are not GreenGo fees.
h) GreenGo invoices the seller for its fee. The seller remains the seller of
   the ticket, and is responsible for the invoice or receipt to the buyer and
   for the taxes on the ticket.

11.7 How many Events and Experiences you can have
a) The limits count the listings that are active at the same time. They are

   - No Membership: you cannot create Events or Experiences.
   - Base: 1 Event and 1 Experience, free only (no paid tickets or bookings).
   - Silver: 5 Events and 5 Experiences.
   - Gold: 10 Events and 10 Experiences.
   - Platinum: unlimited.
b) Draft Experiences count toward the limit.
c) If your Membership changes to a lower plan, your newest listings up to the
   new limit stay visible and the older ones are hidden. Hidden listings are
   not deleted, and they come back if you upgrade again. Tickets and bookings
   already sold stay valid.

11.8 Booking as a guest without an account
a) If the organiser allows guests to book, anyone who opens a shared link to

b) For a paid Event, the guest pays through the same Stripe or Mercado Pago

   attendee names, the guest gives the first name and surname of each ticket

c) Guest booking is not available for Events that need the organiser's
   approval or that have age or gender limits. These Events can be booked

d) Guests can also send a booking request for an Experience from a shared
   link.
e) The guest's name and email, and the names of the ticket holders, are

f) These Terms apply to guest bookings as far as they are relevant.
